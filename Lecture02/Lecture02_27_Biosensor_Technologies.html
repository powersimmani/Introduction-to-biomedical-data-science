Advanced Concepts & Emerging Technologies
Nanostructure Enhancement
Gold Nanoparticles (AuNPs):
• Size: 10-100 nm, tunable plasmon
• Signal amplification via aggregation
• Colorimetric detection (red → blue shift)

Carbon Nanomaterials:
• Graphene: high surface area, conductivity
• CNTs: enhanced electron transfer
• Reduced graphene oxide (rGO): functionalization

Quantum Dots:
• Size-tunable fluorescence (2-10 nm)
• High quantum yield, photostability
• Multiplexed detection capability
Microfluidics Integration

• Automated sample processing

Advantages:
• Reduced reagent consumption
• Faster analysis time
• Portability, multiplexing
• Laminar flow for controlled reactions

Applications:
• Point-of-care diagnostics
• Cell sorting and analysis
• Drug screening platforms
Key Considerations for Biosensor Development:
1. Biocompatibility - Ensure recognition elements maintain activity in operational conditions
2. Stability - Long-term storage (months) and operational lifetime (hours-days)
3. Reproducibility - CV < 5-10% for reliable measurements
4. Calibration - Standard curves, quality control samples
5. Matrix Effects - Validate in real samples (serum, urine, food extracts)
6. Regulatory Approval - FDA/CE mark for clinical applications
