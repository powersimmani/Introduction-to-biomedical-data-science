Summary & Quick Reference
5. Method Selection Quick Reference Guide
Use this quick reference matrix for rapid method selection based on your primary experimental constraint or objective. Each scenario maps to the optimal library preparation approach.
🎯 Budget-Conscious Study
→ 3' Tag-seq
Lowest cost per sample, sufficient for gene-level differential expression, ideal for large sample sets and screening studies.
🎯 Standard Expression Analysis
→ PolyA Selection
Balanced cost-performance, well-established protocols, suitable for most eukaryotic gene expression studies.
🎯 Non-coding RNA / Bacteria
→ Ribosomal Depletion
Captures all RNA types, essential for non-polyadenylated transcripts, prokaryotic samples, and comprehensive transcriptome profiling.
🎯 Isoform Discovery
→ Full-length Coverage
Complete transcript sequences, alternative splicing detection, fusion gene identification, and structural variant discovery.
🎯 Single-cell / Low Input
→ UMI-based Methods
Accurate molecule counting, PCR bias removal, essential for precious samples and single-cell applications.
🎯 Degraded / FFPE Samples
→ Ribo Depletion or 3' Tag-seq
Tolerant to fragmentation, no 3' bias requirement (Ribo Depletion) or leverages 3' end stability (Tag-seq).
