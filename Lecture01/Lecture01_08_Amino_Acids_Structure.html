Peptide Bond Formation

Formation Mechanism

Peptide bonds form through a dehydration synthesis reaction between the carboxyl group (-COOH) of one amino acid and the amino group (-NH₂) of another.


Key Characteristics

• Planar structure due to partial double bond character
• Trans configuration is favored
• Rigid bond restricts rotation
• Forms backbone of all proteins
